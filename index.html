BioTopia

Urban Bio Space Simulator, a serious game made in collaboration with food4future

Growing food the conventional way will get harder in the future, as land, trade and fresh water become scarce. BioTopia shows how a city can still feed itself using alternative sources such as macroalgae, halophytes, jellyfish aquaculture and cricket farming.

Players build compartments into the buildings of a growing city, turn the harvest into dishes like "Medu Mayo" in factories and keep the population fed. As the city grows, so does the available space.

Developed by a team of seven. Before the HTW Showtime exhibition, we presented the game to an expert audience at a food4future event.
